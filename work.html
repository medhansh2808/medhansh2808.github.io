02 / WORLD MODELS

Collaborative work on what driving world models get wrong—and how to fix it.

OBJECT PERMANENCE · OCCUPANCY FORECASTING

Seen, Then Deleted

Why do moving vehicles disappear from a world model’s imagined future? We investigate this failure across three occupancy models and introduce a training-free permanence layer to help keep perceived agents in view.

With Sahil Apage and Kartik Tagore.

Explore the research ↗
CAUSALITY · PLANNING · WORLD MODELS

Plans Are Not Interventions

Changing a driving plan can make a world model move other cars out of the way—hiding a crash that should happen. We measure this plan leakage, study its impact on planning, and investigate repairs using past-only plan information.

With Sahil Apage.

03 / ON MY MIND

A couple of directions I'm exploring. Very much in progress.

↳What if a robot could learn on the job?test-time training · memory · robot policies+

I'm exploring how test-time training can add adaptation and memory to pretrained robot policies. The interesting question for me: what should a robot remember from a demonstration, and how can that change its next action?

I'm particularly interested in tasks that need history, one-shot imitation, and making the training fit on a single GPU.

research exploration
↳Imagining a safe path isn't the same as finding one.world models · trajectory selection · driving+

A driving policy proposes candidate trajectories; a world model predicts what might happen along each one. I'm experimenting with how to choose among them—and how to tell whether the failure came from the candidates or their ranking.

The question I'm chasing: when should we trust an imagined future, and when should uncertainty make us more cautious?
